ABOUT US
We align ourselves with enlightened management philosophy, regarding people as our major assets and working always at maintaining the communication and interpersonal relations. Our management team is caring and future oriented and our planning and programming is organized yet highest possible level of flexible. Our company's commitment to its ethos guarantees our consistently giving our best in any project we undertake

MVCON BUILDERS AND DEVELOPMENT CORPORATION was formed in 2001 with the objective of establishing a sustainable and continually improving construction company which was able to provide predictable and equally constructions to our clients while concurrently fostering lasting relationships with professionals, local councils, government bodies, and suppliers.

We are well equipped to undertake the most technical construction and to direct and manage the works from design to turnover. Whether you require the construction only or you require a full design and construction package, we can manage the process and delivery of the finish product.
MISSION
To excel in our field by building beautiful and structurally sound houses based on the principles of good craftsmanship in construction, techniques, integrity, and customer satisfaction.
VISION
Our vision starts with understanding the needs of our clients so that we can remain responsive, relevant, and competitive in the construction field. This means being adaptive and flexible to the changes related to the business and that we believe will benefit all involved in the operation of the company. We want to be useful and helpful not only to the people that we provide our construction services to but to the people that make up our company as well, this involves building quality resident houses so we can thrive as a business.
